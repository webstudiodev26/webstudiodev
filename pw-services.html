What we offer

Six ways we look after your pet

01

General Veterinary Checkups

Regular veterinary checkups are an important part of caring for a pet's health. During a visit, the animal's overall condition is assessed and its needs are discussed.

02

Vaccination & Prevention

Preventive care covers vaccination, protection from parasites and other measures, adapted to your pet's age, health and lifestyle.

03

Veterinary Diagnostics

Diagnostic procedures help the veterinarian better understand your pet’s condition and judge which next steps are appropriate.

04

Dental Care

The health of teeth and gums is an important part of overall health in dogs and cats. A veterinary exam can help identify problems and plan suitable care.

05

Surgery & Treatment

Veterinary treatment depends on the animal's condition and individual needs. When further intervention is needed, the veterinarian explains the possible procedures and next steps.

Puppy & Kitten Care

The first months of life are important for healthy development. Advice on feeding, prevention and regular checkups helps owners better understand the needs of young animals.
